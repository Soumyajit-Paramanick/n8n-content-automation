# Privacy Policy — n8n Content Automation

**Last updated:** October 11, 2026

## Overview

This project automates the preparation of professional LinkedIn content using n8n and AI services. It is intended for personal and educational use.

## Information Processing

The workflow may process user-provided topics, audience preferences, and writing instructions to generate LinkedIn post drafts. If LinkedIn publishing is enabled, the workflow may also process the content and account authorization information required to publish posts.

## Third-Party Services

This project may use Google Gemini for AI content generation, n8n for workflow automation, and LinkedIn for publishing. These services process information according to their respective privacy policies and terms.

## Data Storage and Security

Access credentials and workflow data should be handled securely. API credentials should not be shared publicly. Data may be retained in the local n8n instance or connected services according to their configurations.

## Data Sharing

This project does not intentionally sell personal information. Information may be transmitted to the third-party services required for the workflow to function.

## Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the project maintainer through their public GitHub profile.

## Policy Updates

This policy may be updated if the workflow's data collection, storage, sharing, or publishing behavior changes.
